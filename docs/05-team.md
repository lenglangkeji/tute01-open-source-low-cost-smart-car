The Tute01 project page credits the **Lenglang Tech Team**, described as being formed by graduate
and undergraduate students from several science-and-engineering and normal universities in Tianjin.
The team states that it focuses on non-standard embedded development, smart-car teaching equipment,
and custom non-standard products.

### The studio

The same team operates **Lenglang Electronics Studio** (冷狼电子工作室), based in Tianjin. The studio
site describes its expertise as STM32/ESP32, Linux drivers, industrial control and IoT, and lists
non-standard embedded development, ROS 2 robot teaching and smart-car teaching kits as its main
activities. See [`06-studio-and-services.md`](06-studio-and-services.md).

### Academic guidance

The studio also runs **疆阔星辉科创比赛指导** (Jiangkuo Xinghui science-and-innovation competition
coaching), which provides competition coaching, postgraduate-recommendation (保研) planning and
paper/research guidance. See [`06-studio-and-services.md`](06-studio-and-services.md).
