## 中文

Tute01 由 **冷狼电子工作室** 发布，而工作室官网 `lenglang.xyz` 正是其生态所在。本页汇总该站点公开发布的内容。这些内容不属于开源智能车代码本身，收录的原因是项目主页把该工作室列为其官网。

### 工作室简介

工作室坐标天津，自述从事**嵌入式开发、工业非标设备定制与 ROS2 机器人教学**。团队自述在 STM32/ESP32、Linux 驱动、工业控制与物联网方向有多年项目经验；除做产品外也带学生，其中包含专门的开源智能车教程，覆盖从零基础到进阶。

宣传能力标签：嵌入式开发、工业非标定制、ROS2 机器人、科创竞赛指导。

### 业务板块

#### 1. 嵌入式产品开发

基于 STM32/ESP32/NXP 等主流 MCU，从原理图设计、PCB Layout 到固件开发、整机调试一条龙。过往案例包括物联网终端、数据采集器、智能控制器等产品。

#### 2. 工业非标自动化

按需定制工控系统：PLC 编程、触摸屏界面、伺服/步进电机运动控制、机器视觉检测。适用于产线自动化改造、检测设备定制、工装夹具智能化等场景。

#### 3. 物联网整体方案

从传感器终端到云平台整套 IoT 方案。支持 WiFi/BLE/LoRa/NB-IoT 等多种无线通信，配套 Android/iOS App、小程序与 Web 端数据监控平台开发。

### 比赛与论文指导

工作室以 **疆阔星辉科创比赛指导** 之名，在天津线下常年承接比赛指导、保研业务以及论文科研指导。该页面称可指导发表 EI 类、SCI 类论文期刊会议等，并声明不涉及学术不端。

## English

Tute01 is published by **Lenglang Electronics Studio**, and the studio website `lenglang.xyz` is
where the surrounding ecosystem lives. This page summarises what that site publishes. None of it is
part of the open-source smart-car code itself; it is included because the project site links to the
studio as its home.

### The studio

Positioned in Tianjin, the studio describes itself as doing **embedded development, industrial
non-standard equipment customisation, and ROS 2 robot teaching**. It says its team has several
years of hands-on experience in STM32/ESP32, Linux drivers, industrial control and IoT, and that in
addition to products it teaches students — including a dedicated open-source smart-car course that
runs from zero background to advanced level.

Advertised capability tags: embedded development, industrial non-standard customisation, ROS 2
robots, science-and-innovation competition coaching.

### Service lines

**1. Embedded product development.** Full chain from schematic design and PCB layout through
firmware development and system-level debugging, based on mainstream MCUs such as STM32, ESP32 and
NXP. Past work cited includes IoT terminals, data loggers and smart controllers.

**2. Industrial non-standard automation.** Custom industrial-control systems: PLC programming, HMI
screens, servo/stepper motion control and machine-vision inspection. Positioned for production-line
automation retrofits, custom inspection equipment and intelligent fixtures.

**3. End-to-end IoT solutions.** Sensor terminals through to cloud platforms. Wireless options
include Wi-Fi, BLE, LoRa and NB-IoT, with accompanying Android/iOS apps, mini-programs and web
monitoring dashboards.

### Competition and paper guidance

Under the name **疆阔星辉科创比赛指导**, the studio offers offline coaching in Tianjin for
competitions, postgraduate-recommendation (保研) planning and paper/research guidance. It states
that it can support publication in EI- and SCI-indexed venues and that the guidance does not
involve academic misconduct. Competitions it says it covers: the China Collegiate Computing Design
Competition, the National Collegiate Embedded Design Competition, the National Collegiate Robot and
AI Competition, the Lanqiao Cup, and emerging-engineering-discipline competitions.

Published figures (as claimed on the site, for project guidance):

| Metric | Claimed value |
| --- | --- |
| National-award rate, national-level projects | above 55% |
| Municipal-award rate, simple projects | above 90% |
| Paper acceptance rate for paper guidance | 98% |

The page notes that the award statistics cover only teams that registered normally and competed.
The studio also states that it cooperates with several postgraduate-exam training institutions in
Tianjin.

> These figures are the publisher claims reproduced from the source site. They are not
> independently verified by this archive.
